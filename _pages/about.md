I am a third-year undergraduate at [Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences](https://iiis.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/) (known as Yao's Class), Tsinghua University. I was a member of the Mathematical Olympiad National Team and represented China in the 11th grade category of *All-Russian Mathematical Olympiad 2019*. Simultaneously, I have won the best paper award in *the 10th International Conference on Computational Data and Social Networks*.

My research interests are mainly causal inference theory, the application of causal inference in social network problems, algorithmic game theory, and online learning. Also, I am familiar with machine learning theory, including reinforcement learning and deep learning.
