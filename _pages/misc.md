in-wheel motors. Our project won the top grade award in the *39th Challenge Cup at Tsinghua University*. See [here](/files/challenge_cup1_2x.jpg) and [here](/files/challenge_cup2_4x.jpg) for pictures. We were granted two patents with Number [CN113153987B](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN113153987B/en) and Number[CN214946139U](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN214946139U/en).
